## 1.0.0

- First release.
- Five stores for Epic Loot materials: Essence Keg, Dust Sack, Reagent Barrel, Shard Bucket and Runestone Chest.
- Each store takes one material family and locks to the first rarity put in.
- Filled stores show a sticker of their contents, with props in the rarity's colour.
- Hover text shows the contents and count.
- Configurable store size (columns and rows), synced from the server.
